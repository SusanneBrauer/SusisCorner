My Professional Journey

From Engineering Problems to Data Solutions

Over the years, I've realised that what I enjoy most about engineering is using data to understand problems and make informed decisions. I've also always enjoyed finding ways to make things work more efficiently.

One of the turning points was developing a measurement dashboard to streamline a particularly time-consuming reporting process. What started as an attempt to make an existing workflow easier gradually sparked a much wider interest in programming, automation and how data is structured and managed.

Along the way, I discovered that I enjoyed building the tools and finding solutions to the underlying data problems just as much as using the information itself.

That realisation is what ultimately led me towards data engineering and software development, where I'm now looking to build on my engineering experience in a new direction.
